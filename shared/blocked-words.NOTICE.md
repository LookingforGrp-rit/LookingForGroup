# Blocked words list: attribution

`blocked-words.en.txt` is the English list from
[List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words),
originally created by Shutterstock and maintained by its contributors. It is
used here unmodified, under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Words the team wants to block in addition go in `blocked-words.custom.txt`, so
the upstream file can be replaced with a newer copy without losing them.
